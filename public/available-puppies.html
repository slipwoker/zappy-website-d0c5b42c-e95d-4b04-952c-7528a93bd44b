How Our Adoption Works

Puppy availability changes as litters grow and families connect with their new companions. Instead of a traditional shopping cart, we invite you to reach out directly. This ensures every placement is thoughtful and every question is answered.

When you inquire about a puppy, you'll speak with Alexia personally. We'll discuss temperament, health records, and what to expect when bringing a tiny Chihuahua into your home. There's no pressure—just a shared love for the breed and a commitment to responsible placement.

Expect a warm, informative conversation. We'll guide you through our care standards, vaccination schedules, and the transition process so your new puppy settles in comfortably from day one.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before welcoming a Chihuahua puppy into your home

What information should I include when reaching out?

To help us match you with the perfect companion, please share a little about your household, daily routine, and experience with small breeds. Let us know if you have other pets, children, or specific preferences for coat color or temperament. The more we know, the better we can guide you toward the right Chihuahua puppy.

When will the puppies be ready to go to their new homes?

Our Chihuahua puppies stay with their mother until they are at least eight to ten weeks old, ensuring they receive proper socialization and nourishment during those critical early weeks. We will keep you updated on developmental milestones and confirm a go-home date as the litter matures.

What kind of home environment do Chihuahua puppies need?

Chihuahuas thrive in calm, affectionate homes where they are treated as part of the family. They adapt well to apartment living but appreciate a secure outdoor space for supervised play. Because of their small size, homes with gentle handling practices and awareness of tiny paws underfoot are ideal.

What health checks do the puppies receive before adoption?

Every puppy receives a thorough veterinary examination, age-appropriate vaccinations, and regular deworming. We monitor for common small-breed concerns such as patellar luxation and dental development, and we provide a written health record so you can continue care seamlessly with your own veterinarian.

What is included when I adopt a Chihuahua puppy?

Your puppy will come with a starter kit that includes a small bag of their current food, a familiar blanket with their littermates' scent, vaccination records, and our care guide covering feeding, grooming, and early training tips for Chihuahua puppy sales. We also remain available for questions long after you take your puppy home.

What are the next steps after I submit an inquiry?

Once we receive your message, we will reply within 24 to 48 hours to schedule a relaxed phone conversation. This gives us a chance to learn about each other, answer your questions, and discuss available or upcoming litters. If it feels like a good fit, we will arrange a visit or video call so you can meet the puppies.
