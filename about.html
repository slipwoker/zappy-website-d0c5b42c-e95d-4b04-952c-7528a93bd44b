Alexia's Story

With over a decade of experience in professional Chihuahua breeding, Alexia Phipps has dedicated her life to raising healthy, well-socialized puppies in a nurturing home environment. Her journey began with a deep love for this spirited breed and a commitment to ethical breeding practices that prioritize temperament and health above all else.

Every Chihuahua puppy available for adoption is raised underfoot in Alexia's family home, surrounded by gentle handling, early neurological stimulation, and plenty of affection. This approach ensures each puppy transitions smoothly into their new family, carrying with them the confidence and warmth that only a caring start can provide. Alexia personally oversees every aspect of care—from nutrition to socialization—so that when you welcome one of her puppies, you're welcoming a well-loved companion.

Our Standards & Care

Every puppy raised with love, integrity, and the highest ethical standards.

Ethical Breeding Standards

We adhere to rigorous health and genetic screening protocols to ensure every Chihuahua puppy is born with the best possible start in life. Our breeding program prioritises temperament, longevity, and conformation, following guidelines that exceed standard kennel club requirements. This commitment to quality in chihuahua puppy sales means you bring home a companion bred with care, not volume.

Gentle Socialisation Approach

From the moment they open their eyes, our puppies are handled with patience and kindness. They are raised in a warm home environment, exposed to everyday household sounds, gentle children, and other pets. This early socialisation builds confident, well-adjusted companions who transition smoothly into their forever families, reflecting our belief that a well-raised chihuahua yorkie mix puppy or purebred deserves the gentlest start.

Cleanliness & Care Commitment

Our nursery and play areas are maintained to immaculate standards, with daily cleaning routines, fresh bedding, and regular veterinary check-ups. We believe a healthy environment is the foundation of a healthy puppy. Prospective families are welcome to see our setup and witness first-hand the dedication that goes into raising great pyrenees puppies and our tiny Chihuahua treasures alike, ensuring complete transparency in our care practices.
